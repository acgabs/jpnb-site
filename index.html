JPNB – Just Photos. No Bloat.

JPNB ist eine schlanke Android-App für Fotos mit Notizen und #Tags – für Menschen die ihre Fotos nicht automatisch in die Hände der großen Konzerne geben wollen und lieber eigenverantwortlich leben.

JPNB – Just Photos. No Bloat.

JPNB is a lightweight Android app for photos with notes and #Tags — built for people who do not want to hand their fotos to big tech automatically and who want to keep more control over their life.

  • Local storage: JPNB stores photos, notes, and tags locally in private app storage and does not transmit personal content to cloud services.
  • No clutter: No user account, no tracking, no ads.
  • Temporary photos: Optional expiration reminders for photos you don't want to keep forever.

Transparency note: This app was created by AI based on my ideas and specifications (the app itself runs 100% offline with no AI involved). If that is a problem for you, you should not download it.
